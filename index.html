About NovaTrade

NovaTrade is a cryptocurrency market-monitoring dashboard designed for mobile and desktop. It displays public market information when the data provider is reachable.

Important: This version is a market-watch website only. It does not connect to a broker, execute trades, accept deposits, or process withdrawals. Prices are informational and may be delayed or unavailable. Cryptocurrency trading carries financial risk.
